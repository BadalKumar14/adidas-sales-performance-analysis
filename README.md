# 👟 Adidas Sales Performance Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes comprehensive Adidas retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue drivers, operating profitability, regional distribution, and sales channel effectiveness across the United States.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze total sales revenue and operating profit across multiple dimensions.
- Identify top-performing product categories and footwear/apparel lines.
- Compare sales performance across different sales channels (In-store, Outlet, Online).
- Evaluate regional sales distribution and top-performing states/cities.
- Assess retailer contributions and sales trends over time.
- Standardize and structure transactional datasets for business reporting.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Software:** Microsoft Excel
- **Features Used:** Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Formulas, and Interactive Dashboards

---

## 📊 Key Metrics & Concepts Used
- **Total Sales Revenue:** $899,902,125.00
- **Total Operating Profit:** $332,134,761.45
- **Total Units Sold:** 2,478,861 units
- **Average Operating Margin:** ~42.3%
- **Data Aggregation & Summarization:** SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT
- **Pivot Tables & Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by region, retailer, and product line

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

1. **Sales Channel Performance:**
   * **In-store** sales generate the highest revenue contribution at **356,643,750.00**, followed by **Outlet** sales at **295,585,493.00** and **Online** sales at **247,672,882.00**.
2. **Product Categories:**
   * **Men's Street Footwear** leads total sales at **208,826,244.00**, closely followed by **Women's Apparel** at **179,038,860.00**.
3. **Regional Performance:**
   * The **West** region records the highest regional sales at **269,943,182.00**, significantly outperforming other regions.
4. **Top Retailers:**
   * **West Gear** is the top-performing retail partner generating **242,964,333.00**, closely followed by **Foot Locker** at **220,094,720.00**.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory investments on high-demand categories like Men's Street Footwear and Women's Apparel.
- Optimize digital and online sales channels to capture broader market share alongside strong In-store performance.
- Evaluate regional marketing strategies to replicate the high sales success seen in the West region across lower-performing regions.
- Strengthen strategic partnerships with top-performing retailers like West Gear and Foot Locker.
